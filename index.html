Yassin - Ramadan

A private personal assistant used by one person: the owner of this app.

Yassin is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. With the owner's permission it reads statistics of the owner's own YouTube channel (read-only), to help him plan and review his content.

It does not upload, edit or delete videos, does not post comments, and does not access any other person's channel or data. It is not offered to the public.
