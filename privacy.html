Privacy Policy

Yassin - Ramadan · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who uses this app

Yassin is a private assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is accessed.

What data it accesses

  • With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API with the read-only scope youtube.readonly.
  • It reads the owner's own channel information: channel name, subscriber, view and video counts, and video titles and statistics.
  • It cannot upload, edit or delete videos, or post anything.

How the data is used

  • To show the owner his channel figures and growth, and to help him plan and review his content.
  • To produce these reports, channel statistics may be sent to the AI model provider the app uses (currently DeepSeek). They are sent only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Where the data is stored

  • The OAuth token and any channel data are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Nothing is stored on a public server.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time from his Google Account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the local token file removes the app's access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the support email shown on the Google consent screen of this app.
